## Executive Summary

This roadmap outlines Stegano's comprehensive enterprise compliance implementation strategy over 24 months, establishing the framework for SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, FedRAMP, and FIPS 140-2 compliance. The phased approach balances speed-to-market with thoroughness, enabling enterprise customer acquisition while building long-term compliance excellence.

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives

### Business Goals
- **Market Access**: Unlock $2.8B regulated market opportunities
- **Customer Trust**: Enable Fortune 500 and government sales
- **Competitive Advantage**: Differentiate through compliance excellence
- **Risk Mitigation**: Reduce legal, regulatory, and operational risks
- **Valuation Impact**: Increase company valuation through compliance assets

### Compliance Targets
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Q4 2025 certification
- **ISO 27001**: Q1 2026 certification
- **NIST CSF**: Level 3 (Repeatable) by Q4 2025
- **FedRAMP Ready**: Q2 2026 assessment

- ✅ **Security** (Required): 100% implementation target
- ✅ **Availability** (Selected): 99.9% uptime objective
- ✅ **Processing Integrity** (Selected): Statistical validation integration
- ✅ **Confidentiality** (Selected): FIPS 140-2 cryptography
- ✅ **Privacy** (Selected): GDPR/CCPA compliance alignment

#### Key Milestones
- **Q1 2025**: Gap analysis and remediation plan
- **Q2 2025**: Control implementation and documentation
- **Q3 2025**: Internal testing and SOC 2 Type I
- **Q4 2025**: Operating effectiveness period and Type II certification

### ISO 27001 Implementation

#### ISMS Development
- **Risk Assessment**: Comprehensive information security risk assessment
- **Risk Treatment**: 114 Annex A controls implementation
- **Continuous Improvement**: PDCA cycle implementation
- **Management Review**: Quarterly management reviews

#### Implementation Phases
- **Q1 2025**: ISMS planning and design
- **Q2 2025**: Control implementation
- **Q3 2025**: Internal audit and management review
- **Q1 2026**: External certification audit

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework

#### Maturity Progression
- **Current State**: Level 1 (Partial)
- **Q4 2025 Target**: Level 3 (Repeatable)
- **Q4 2026 Target**: Level 4 (Adaptive)

#### Function Implementation
- **Identify**: Asset management and risk assessment
- **Protect**: Access control and data protection
- **Detect**: Continuous monitoring and threat detection
- **Respond**: Incident response and recovery
- **Recover**: Business continuity and lessons learned

### FedRAMP Preparation

#### Authorization Strategy
- **Impact Level**: Low (initial), Moderate (future)
- **Deployment Model**: Cloud Service Provider (CSP)
- **Authorization Path**: Joint Authorization Board (JAB)

#### NIST SP 800-53 Controls
- **Low Impact**: 325 controls implementation
- **Documentation**: System Security Plan (SSP) development
- **Assessment**: Independent assessment organization (3PAO)
- **Timeline**: 12-18 months for full authorization

### FIPS 140-2 Validation

#### Cryptographic Module Requirements
- **Security Level**: Level 3 (tamper-evident)
- **Algorithm Testing**: CAVP validation required
- **Module Testing**: CMVP validation process
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months for full validation

#### Implementation Approach
- **Q2 2026**: Submit for algorithm validation
- **Q3 2026**: Begin module validation testing
- **Q1 2027**: Complete validation and certification
